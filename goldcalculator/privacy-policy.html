Privacy Policy

Effective date: 09/08/2026

Gold Bill Calculator ("the app") is developed and maintained by SAN ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. The app requires no account, no sign-up, and no login. All calculator inputs (weight, rate, making charges, discount, GST, old-gold exchange) and your settings (visual style, accent color, GST default, display precision) are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us or to any server we operate. The app does not require an internet connection to calculate a bill.

We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or any other data-collection tool of our own.

Advertising

The app displays banner ads via Google AdMob to support free distribution. Google (and its advertising partners) may collect and process information — such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and app usage/interaction data — to serve, measure, and personalize ads. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under its own privacy policy; we do not receive or store it. Details: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices. Where required by law (including the EEA, UK, and certain US states), the app asks for your consent — via Google's User Messaging Platform — before showing personalized ads; you can decline and still use the app. You can review or reset your ad personalization choices for your device at any time in Google's Ads Settings or your device's system ad settings (e.g. Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share any data with third parties beyond Google's ad-serving described above.

Children's privacy

This app is a general-purpose billing/estimation tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

Your rights

Because the app itself stores your data only on your own device and nothing is sent to us, there is nothing on our servers to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about data Google's advertising services process on your device, you can manage it directly via the links in Advertising above, or through Google's own privacy controls at myaccount.google.com. Residents of the EEA/UK and California may also have rights under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA respectively regarding data processed by Google as described above; see Google's privacy resources linked in this policy for how to exercise them.

Data retention & security

We do not operate any server or database for this app, so we do not retain any user data. On-device data (your inputs and settings) stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if the app's advertising integration changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
